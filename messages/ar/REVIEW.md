## Notes for Reviewer

1. **RTL**: Arabic is right-to-left. Arrows in strings like "← Back to store" have been flipped to "العودة إلى المتجر →" to match RTL reading direction.
2. **Technical terms**: Brand names and technical identifiers (CipherPay, Zcash, ZEC, API, UFVK, etc.) are kept in English, which is standard practice in Arabic tech localization.
3. **Placeholders**: Variables like `{amount}`, `{time}`, `{store}` are preserved exactly as-is.
4. **Tone**: Formal but accessible Modern Standard Arabic, consistent with how Google/Apple localize their products.
5. **Full FAQ answers**: The FAQ answers are lengthy. The complete translations are in the `faq.json` file — please review those as well.

Thank you for reviewing!
